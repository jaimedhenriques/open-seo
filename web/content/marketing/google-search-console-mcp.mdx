Do not add the hosted endpoint to an MCP client yet. Check [launch status](/get-started) for the current verification checklist.

## The intended hosted flow

Once the hosted beta opens, the connection is designed to work in three steps:

1. Create a SearchCrew workspace.
2. Connect a Search Console property with read-only permission.
3. Add the verified SearchCrew MCP endpoint to your AI client and authorize access.

The final endpoint and client-specific commands will be published only after DNS, authentication, recovery, and authorization checks pass.

If you need access sooner, review the [self-hosting guide](/docs/self-hosting). Self-hosting requires your own Google OAuth client and infrastructure.

## What the integration is designed to read

The implementation includes two read-only tools over the property you connect.

**Search performance.** Clicks, impressions, CTR, and average position for your site, sliced by query, page, country, device, and date. Pull up to 16 months of history in a single question.

**URL inspection.** Whether a page is indexed and, if not, why: coverage status, last crawl, the canonical Google chose versus the one you declared, plus mobile and rich-result checks. Up to 10 URLs at a time.

The requested Google scope is read-only. The integration is not designed to change your Search Console account.

## FAQ

### What does it cost?

Hosted billing is not open. Review [pricing](/pricing) for the proposed tiers and [launch status](/get-started) for readiness.

### Do I need to give SearchCrew my Google password?

No. The connection uses Google OAuth, so you sign in with Google and grant read-only Search Console access. SearchCrew never sees your credentials, and you can revoke access from your Google account at any time.

### Is it read-only?

Yes. SearchCrew requests read-only access (`webmasters.readonly`). Your agent can read performance data and inspect URLs, but it can't change your account.

### Which AI clients work?

The intended hosted flow targets MCP clients including Claude Code, Claude Desktop, Cursor, Codex, and OpenClaw. Verified connection commands will be published when the beta opens.

### How fresh is the data?

It's Google's own data, so the most recent few days can be incomplete. History runs back 16 months, the same as the Search Console interface.
