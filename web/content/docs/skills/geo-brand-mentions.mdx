The AI Brand Mentions Agent Skill checks what assistants actually say about your brand, and which sources they lean on to say it.

Rankings tell you where you sit in a results page. This tells you something different: whether you are named at all when someone asks an assistant about your category, and whose pages are shaping that answer.

## What this skill helps your agent do

- Ask the questions your buyers ask, and record how each assistant answers.
- Capture which sources are cited — often competitors, review sites, or forums rather than you.
- Track how the description of your brand shifts over time.
- Identify which pages you would need to own for the answer to change.

## When to use it

Run it as a baseline before AI-visibility work, then again after changes land, so you can see whether the answers moved.

## What you get back

A per-assistant record of what was said and what was cited, with the gap between how you describe yourself and how you are being described.
