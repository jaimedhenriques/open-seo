The GEO Content Agent Skill drafts and rewrites content for generative engines: answer-first structure, self-contained passages, and the fact density that makes a quote worth lifting.

## What this skill helps your agent do

- Open sections with a direct answer rather than a warm-up paragraph.
- Keep passages self-contained, so they survive being quoted without surrounding context.
- Add the specifics — figures, dates, named entities — that make a passage worth citing.
- Restructure existing content without losing the voice it already has.

## When to use it

After [AI Citability](/docs/skills/geo-citability) has scored a page and shown you which sections are weak.

## What you get back

Drafted or rewritten sections you can paste in, with the reasoning for each structural change.
