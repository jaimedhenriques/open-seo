## Public signup is paused

SearchCrew is not currently accepting public hosted accounts, email-list submissions, or payments.

The final privacy notice will be published before those services open. It will identify the legal operator and data controller, explain what data is collected and why, list the service providers involved, define retention periods, provide an owner-controlled contact channel, and explain the rights available to users in the markets SearchCrew serves.

This page is a launch-status notice. It is not the final privacy notice for the hosted service.

The free [SEO ROI calculator](/seo-roi-calculator) does not require an account and performs its calculations in your browser.
